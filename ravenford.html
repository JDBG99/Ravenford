Welcome to Ravenford Alley!

This neighborhood was abandoned at the early 50's due to the many disappearances that took place there. According to the research of Michael Schmidt, The Blackwwod Committee kidnaped them for their experiments, it was practically in no use, nobody came to clean the street up, the lights weren't on at night. At the back of the alley, the gothic cathedral locates, in the middle of a park. You can also look a the scaffolding surrounding the back of the building, it is actually a secret laboratory for The Blackwood Committee, a place they called "The Yellow Tower".

1097-1280: The original building

The name of the alley is the same name of the city, its name was given by an old medieval tradition. The cathedral at the back of the alley was pretty different from what we can see nowadays. At first, it was built in a romanesque style, and the bell tower was even taller than the current one. Unfortunately we don't have any graphic evidence of how it looked like.

1292: The Fire

In 1292, the person in charge of cleaning the bells felt from the tower, people think that he wanted to end his own life, since then, the tower was named after "The tower of suicide". The same year, a society invaded the town by surprise, a society dedicated to worship a false idol. During the day, the pagans took chaos within the town. The cathedral was severely damaged, most of the stained glass were lost, even the tower was severely affected by the flames. In a few hours, the cathedral caught on fire

1300-1458: Gothic reform

After the fire, the cathedral was in repair, this time replacing the old romanesque style by the new movement: Gothic architecture. The new "tower of suicide" was given a new spire, along with a clock, only two of the original bells were recovered from the ruins.

1950's-2003: The underground laboratory and the scaffolding

The scaffolding tower was placed at the early 50's, many kids were disappearing a the alley close to the cathedral, since then, the scaffolding never left. According to Michael Schmidt, the scaffolding had a secret PIN code to acces the underground Blackwood Committee laboratory taking the elevator.

2005-2012: The Great Earthquake

In 2005, Ravenford was severely damaged by an earthquake, many buildings were affected, including the cathedral. Luckily it wasn't that bad, only the spire felt from the top, the tower could stand yet, but of course it needed rehabilitation. Many people were discusing either to build a new spire or not because of heritage policy. In the end, in 2012 the new spire was finished
